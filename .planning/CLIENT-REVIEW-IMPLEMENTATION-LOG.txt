================================================================================
1. WHAT THE CLIENT HAD REVIEWED (PRE-CHANGE STATE)
================================================================================

Before this cycle, the site still read as a polished marketing/template product,
not as the digital headquarters of a structural / industrial engineering practice.

Homepage journey they saw (approximate):
  Hero (busy)
    → About / trust matrix style blocks
    → Projects
    → Services (10 coordinated disciplines framing)
    → Construction Sequence (6-stage interactive “signature” engine)
    → Sectors grid
    → Lead / CTA strip
    → Large stats / “FORMX by Numbers”
    → Testimonials / client feedback
    → Footer

Problems the founder marked (themes across PDFs):
  • Too much marketing language and template UI (cards, pills, numbered nav).
  • Hero clutter: CAD overlay toggle, rotating marketing headlines, project
    slide card, stats visible immediately on open.
  • Wrong contact / GREENFIELD label in header.
  • Sectors in primary navigation.
  • MEP listed everywhere; public story should be Architecture · Structure · Infra.
  • Dummy / invented content (fake blogs, news, career roles, client logos,
    practice-lead cards).
  • About page: photo background, crumbs, stats bar, methodology card stacks,
    design-feature “Why Choose” cards instead of human values.
  • Construction Sequence section crossed out entirely on home.
  • Featured Portfolio grid of non-brochure / wrong framing crossed out.
  • Testimonials + dark “Track Record of Industrial Scale” stats crossed out.
  • Footer copy and mega wordmark not matching brochure / Jacobs-scale ask.
  • Need exact brochure language and real completed projects from FORMX.pdf.
